This is a hypothetical illustration, not a quote or an offer. Every figure follows from assumptions you selected on this page. It does not represent any specific annuity contract, and no carrier has agreed to these terms.

The benefit base is not an account value. It is a calculation figure used to determine lifetime income. It cannot be withdrawn as a lump sum, is generally not payable as a death benefit, and has no cash value.

Index credits are not guaranteed. The assumed credit rate drives the account value line only. Actual credits depend on index performance and on caps, participation rates and spreads set by the carrier, which can change.

Rider charges are typically deducted from the account value and continue whether or not the index credits anything, which is why the account value can decline in flat years. Guarantees are backed by the claims-paying ability of the issuing insurer.
