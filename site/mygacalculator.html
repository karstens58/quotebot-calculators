Good to Know

MYGAs typically allow penalty-free withdrawals of up to 10% of the account value each year. Withdrawing more than that before the term ends may trigger a surrender charge, which declines each year until it reaches zero at maturity. Surrender schedules vary by carrier — we'll show you the exact terms for the products in your quote.
