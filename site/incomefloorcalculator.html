How the guaranteed income is built

A Fixed Indexed Annuity with a Guaranteed Lifetime Withdrawal Benefit (GLWB) works in three stages:

1
Fund a one-time premium today from a portion of your portfolio.
2
Grow the income base by a guaranteed roll-up during your 10 deferral years.
3
Receive a guaranteed monthly check at retirement — for life, regardless of markets.
