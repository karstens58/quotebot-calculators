Step 2 of 5
Right-Size — design a benefit that fills your gap

The goal is precision, not maximum coverage. Rather than purchasing the most coverage possible, the C.A.R.E. model targets the benefit that protects your most vulnerable assets — and nothing more.

Tip: If a couple has $400,000 in retirement savings and estimates a 3-year care event could consume $300,000, a policy providing a $300,000 lifetime benefit pool is the target. Costs above that threshold are self-funded.
