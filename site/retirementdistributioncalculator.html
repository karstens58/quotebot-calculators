Life Insurance Tax Strategy — Comparing Outcomes
In this strategy, your qualified account distributes only the exact income you need (no gross-up). The tax owed on that distribution is paid by life insurance cash value — preserving more of your retirement accounts. The chart and table compare both strategies side by side.
How this works
Standard Strategy (Strategy A)Qualified accounts are grossed up — you withdraw more than you need so taxes are paid from the account itself. Each dollar of tax reduces the compounding balance.
Life Insurance Strategy (Strategy B)Qualified accounts distribute only your target income. The tax bill is paid from life insurance cash value — keeping your qualified balance larger and compounding longer. The LI pool grows at a fixed 1.0% net rate (4.5% gross minus 3.5% expenses), independent of market returns.

Strategy B assumes life insurance cash value is available to pay income taxes on qualified distributions each year; the LI pool grows and declines at a fixed 1.0% net rate (4.5% gross minus 3.5% expenses), independent of market returns.
