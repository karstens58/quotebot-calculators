The M.U.G. Method
Simple. Focused. Surprisingly Affordable.
01
Identify Your Non-Negotiables

The M.U.G. strategy focuses on just three expenses — Mortgage, Utilities, and Groceries. These are the bills that cannot be skipped without losing your home or going without food. Everything else can be reduced, deferred, or renegotiated in a crisis. These cannot.

02
Set a 24-Month Benefit Window

Most disability situations resolve — through recovery, adaptation, or qualification for longer-term programs like SSDI — within two years. A 24-month benefit period dramatically lowers your premium compared to lifetime or to-age-65 coverage, while still protecting your household through the most financially dangerous window.

03
Pay Less Than 1% of Your Income

Because the benefit amount is targeted and the benefit period is defined, M.U.G. policies typically cost well under 1% of annual income per year. For many households, that's less per day than a cup of coffee — for protection that could mean the difference between keeping or losing your home during the hardest months of your life.
